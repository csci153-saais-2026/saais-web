Change password

Update the password for your student account.

Your password

You'll need your current password to set a new one.

  • At least 12 characters
  • Contains a letter and a number
  • Different from your current password
  • Both new password fields match
If you sign in with Continue with Google, your password is managed by Google and this page does not change it.
Changing your password signs you out of every other device. The change is written to the audit log; the password itself never is.
