POST/functions/v1/students/{student_id}/record-grades

recordGrades

Records an enrollment or missing grades for one course offering. Checks strict prerequisites at commit; with continue_with_prerequisite_override and a reason, the override and its audit entry are written in one transaction. Locked terms reject the write unless an override applies.

bearerAuthRole: adviser (current adviser only)Contract v0.3.0

Request body

FieldTypeRequiredNotes
school_term_iduuidyesTerm being recorded.
course_offering_iduuidyesMust belong to that term.
midterm_gradenumberno1.00–5.00 in 0.25 steps.
final_gradenumberno1.00–5.00 in 0.25 steps.
instructorstringno
continue_with_prerequisite_overridebooleannoDefault false.
prerequisite_override_reasonstringconditionalRequired when the override is true.

Responses

200Grades recorded.
409Strict prerequisite not met without override, term locked, or a grade already recorded.
403Caller is not the student's current adviser.
400Invalid grade or input.
