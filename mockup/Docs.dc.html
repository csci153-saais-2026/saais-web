POST/functions/v1/enrollment-attempts

recordEnrollmentAttempt

Records a student attempt against a course offering. Runs the strict-prerequisite soft check. When prerequisite_override is true, the attempt and its AuditLogEntry are written in a single transaction.

bearerAuthRole: adviser, adminContract v1.4.0

Request body

FieldTypeRequiredNotes
student_iduuidyesMust be an actively assigned advisee.
course_offering_iduuidyesOffering must belong to an unlocked school term.
curriculum_term_course_iduuidnoSet when the attempt fills a checklist slot.
prerequisite_overridebooleannoDefaults to false; true forces the audit write.
override_reasonstringconditionalRequired when the override flag is true.

Responses

201Attempt recorded.
409Strict prerequisite not satisfied and no override supplied.
403Caller is not the student's active adviser.
423The school term is locked.
