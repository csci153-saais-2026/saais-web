How this GWA is computed

Sum of (final grade × units) divided by total units, across every counting attempt. A retake under grade replacement supersedes its earlier attempt; a course marked additional credit counts every passed attempt separately. Unresolved INCs past their deadline are evaluated as 5.00 without changing the recorded status — this is a read-time rule, not a stored transition.

Σ(grade × units) ÷ Σ units
102.50 ÷ 61 = 1.68
