# OpenAPI 0.3.1 candidate

Status: **proposed; not frozen**. The project owner changed the curriculum cloning rule so Department Staff and Department Heads can clone when both source and target are within their assigned department. Admin-only accounts still require an independently assigned in-scope Head or Staff role to clone.

The previous approved baseline remains [OpenAPI 0.3.0](FREEZE-0.3.0.md). This authorization expansion changes behavior and must not be presented as covered by the 0.3.0 approvals.
