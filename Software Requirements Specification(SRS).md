# Software Requirements Specification — SAAIS

## 1. Purpose, authority and scope

SAAIS is a production Student Academic Advising Information System for one institution. It helps administrators manage academic structures and accounts, advisers manage assigned students, and students view their own records. It records advising enrollment and grades; it does not perform registrar enrollment.

The interface reference is `mockup/prototype.html`, its route map and the role pages and shared assets it loads; `mockup/PORTAL-SYNC.md` describes the current presentation. Historical generators and orphaned assets are not runtime evidence. Explicit project decisions override prototype defects. `README.md` describes the intended production application; this SRS records detailed development requirements. Browser-only storage, scripted replies and role switching demonstrate workflows and are not production authorization or persistence.

The separate enrollment system, student emails requesting enrollment approval, and adviser email replies remain outside SAAIS. After approving enrollment externally, an adviser records the approved courses here. Routine actions do not require administrator approval.

## 5. Courses, grading scale, school terms and offerings

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| FR-CRS-01 | The Department Staff course catalogue (`/department-staff/course-catalog`) supports authorized course management: unique code, title, administering Department (`department_id`), units (positive numeric), course type (`non_elective` or `elective`), status (`active` or `discontinued`, shown in UI as Active/Inactive) without a repeat-policy selector. |
| FR-CRS-02 | Different course codes never imply equivalence. Any student-specific decision (FR-EQV-01) or explicitly approved curriculum-pair mapping (FR-EQV-08) must be recorded and scoped; a code match alone does not create a global equivalence. |
| FR-CRS-03 | Prerequisites have `strict` (labelled `Prerequisite` in catalog UI), `co_requisite` (`Co-requisite`) or `recommended` type. Strict prerequisites determine clearance and trigger the FR-ENR-12 override warning; other types are advisory and do not prevent recording. |
| FR-CRS-04 | Numeric grades use the 1.00–5.00 scale in discrete 0.25 increments (`1.00` to `3.00` in `0.25` steps are Passed; `> 3.00`, notably `5.00`, is Failed), alongside non-numeric attempt statuses `INC`, `DR` and `NA`. Midterm grades do not determine final status or GWA. |
| FR-CRS-05 | School terms are identified by `school_year` and normalized `term_type`, with dates and server-derived closure metadata. Department Heads and Department Staff close and reopen grade encoding for their own department; System Administrators can manage department closures and the institution-wide lock. For each student and term, the closure of the student’s current department controls grade-changing writes even when the course offering belongs to a different department. That closure blocks those writes until reopened, except the first completion of an unresolved INC before or on its deadline under FR-GWA-02. Only a System Administrator may directly toggle the institution-wide lock override; each change is audited. The current prototype has no override control. |
| FR-CRS-06 | Locked terms block enrollment, initial grade entry, grade/status corrections and prerequisite-grade overrides unless an authorized term override applies. **Exception: an unresolved INC may be completed on or before its deadline even when its term is institution-locked or the department has closed encoding.** Editing an existing INC completion is a correction and obeys both the institution-wide lock and department closure. |
| FR-CRS-07 | Every CourseOffering references a school term and course and indicates whether it is `is_by_request`. Active-term course offerings ("Opened subjects") are displayed and managed within the Academic Terms / Course Offerings workflow, filterable by curriculum version and year standing with total opened units. Availability must be explicit; courses are not assumed offered every term. |
| FR-CRS-08 | Discontinued required courses may have by-request offerings for older curricula. |
| FR-CRS-09 | Curriculum terms are positional requirements, distinct from calendar school terms. Flexible slots (`Elective slot` or `GE slot`, modeled with `is_elective_slot = true`) may have no fixed course and carry nominal units. |
| FR-CRS-10 | School-term types are 1st Semester, 2nd Semester and Summer/Midyear. UI labels may use Summer/Midyear while stored term type is normalized consistently. |

## 6. Record grades: enrollment and first-time grade entry

The Record grades (`Encode grades`) workflow records courses already approved in the institution's separate enrollment process and encodes initial midterm, final or INC-completion grades for a selected advisee and school term. Editing an already-saved grade value requires the audited grade-correction workflow in §7.

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| FR-ENR-01 | Record grades (`/adviser/encode-grades`, `/adviser/advisees/:id/record-grades` and `/adviser/enrollment/new`) is an Adviser workflow for a selected current advisee and school term. Enrollment and grade writes are declared contract operations and transactionally enforced server-side. The six advisee-record views are listed in FR-ADV-07. |
| FR-ENR-02 | One Attempt per student + course offering. Retakes use another offering and preserve all earlier attempts; reject a new attempt after the student has passed the same course, including by INC completion. |
| FR-ENR-03 | An attempt may directly fulfill one matching curriculum slot (`curriculum_term_course_id`); this optional link is distinct from elective mapping and equivalency. |
| FR-ENR-04 | Attempts carry status (`passed`, `failed`, `currently_enrolled`, `inc`, `dr`, `na`), optional midterm/final grades, an optional instructor name recorded per attempt, INC deadline when applicable, timestamps and audited correction/override provenance. The instructor name is retained with that attempt and visible in authorized course-attempt detail; it does not model instructor scheduling. |
| FR-ENR-05 | A student may have no enrollment records or records in multiple school terms, with one or more courses per recorded term. |
| FR-ENR-06 | Record grades provides a student and school-term selector and a multi-course term grid with course, instructor, midterm, final and staff-only remark fields, plus an inline pending-INC resolution panel. The adviser can add an offered course or choose an offered elective for an elective slot, and can remove unsaved newly staged rows before saving (persisted attempts cannot be deleted; dropping uses `DR` under FR-GRD-03). The selected offering must belong to the selected school term and its applicable lock is enforced; changing student or term discards unsaved edits and never submits stale data. Cross-program records may be unattached to a curriculum slot pending an explicit mapping/equivalency. |
| FR-ENR-07 | Adding a new course offering with both grades blank records `currently_enrolled`. Submitting a duplicate new enrollment for an offering the student already has in that term is rejected with an already-enrolled message and performs no write. |
| FR-ENR-08 | Recording a new enrollment that already includes a midterm grade requires explicit adviser confirmation (or confirmation of the batch save) explaining that it will enroll the student and record the supplied grades. Cancel performs no write. |
| FR-ENR-09 | A supplied final grade determines Passed (`<= 3.00`) or Failed (`> 3.00`) immediately; no final grade (and non-INC/DR/NA status) means `currently_enrolled`. For example, midterm 5.00 + final 3.00 is Passed, and final 3.25 or 5.00 is Failed. |
| FR-ENR-10 | For an existing attempt loaded in the Record grades grid, blank inputs or unchanged existing grade values leave stored grades untouched (idempotent), while empty (`NULL`) stored fields (`instructor`, `midterm_grade`, `final_grade`, `remarks`) may be populated and saved atomically. Attempting to overwrite an already-populated stored grade with a different value through Record grades rejects the entire submission with no partial save and directs the adviser to Grades (§7) so a correction reason is recorded. An existing completion grade counts as an existing final result. |
| FR-ENR-11 | Example: an existing attempt with midterm 2.00 and final empty accepts a submission where midterm remains unchanged (or blank) and final is 1.75, saving the final grade. Submitting a modified midterm (e.g. 1.50) or explicitly re-submitting a conflicting grade value in a single-attempt endpoint rejects the entire request with no partial save. |
| FR-ENR-12 | When adding or saving a course whose strict prerequisite is unmet, display the missing requirement(s) in a modal and offer **Continue with override** or **Cancel**. Continue requires a non-empty adviser-provided reason and atomically records `prerequisite_override = true`, the reason and an `AuditLogEntry` with the attempt; Cancel makes no enrollment write. Co-requisite and recommended prerequisites remain advisory. |
| FR-ENR-13 | Only the current adviser may record attempts or initial grades; historical advisers cannot mutate records. Validate offering membership, duplicate keys, grade increments, DR pairing, term lock and preconditions again at commit time inside a database transaction to prevent race-condition overwrites. |
| FR-ENR-14 | The grade grid supports numeric grades alongside `INC`, `DR` and `NA`. Selecting `DR` for a midterm automatically sets the final to `DR`; selecting `DR` for a final requires a `DR` midterm (otherwise it is rejected/cleared with a validation message), and changing the midterm away from `DR` clears a `DR` final. Initial `INC` recording sets `status = inc` and computes `inc_deadline` per FR-GWA-01; existing unresolved `INC` attempts for the advisee can be resolved with a completion grade and required reason either from Grades (`/adviser/advisees/:id/grades`) or from the Record grades pending-INC panel, invoking the same transactional `INCResolution` operation (FR-GWA-02). |

## 7. Grades, corrections, dropping and prerequisite clearance

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| FR-GRD-01 | `/adviser/advisees/:id/grades` shows school terms newest first, then term grades and GWA. Student Grades uses the same chronological order and student-safe data. |
| FR-GRD-02 | The current adviser may correct grades/status, including DR or NA, in Grades when the term is editable. Corrections retain actor, reason, timestamp and old/new values in audit history. Passed/Failed derives from the final grade, not midterm. |
| FR-GRD-03 | Dropping means changing a course attempt to DR; it does not remove the student from the advisee roster. Advisers may correct/reverse course statuses through Grades subject to lock and INC rules. Registrar enrollment is unaffected. |
| FR-GRD-04 | A correction to a failed prerequisite's original final grade requires a passing replacement grade and a reason. It is not a grade for the dependent course and does not require admin approval. This grade correction is distinct from the enrollment warning override in FR-ENR-12. |
| FR-GRD-05 | A prerequisite-grade override updates the original final grade and Passed status atomically with its audit entry, recomputes GWA/standing, and clears the prerequisite for all downstream courses. The student sees the new grade; staff-only reasons are not exposed as advising notes. Original values remain in audit history. |
| FR-GRD-06 | A failed prerequisite remains unfulfilled until a passing retake, valid equivalency, or authorized passing grade correction clears it. An enrollment override does not change the prerequisite grade or mark it passed. There is no fabricated course-code equivalence or automatic pass. |

## 8. INC, GWA and delinquency

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| FR-GWA-01 | INC has a compliance deadline, default one calendar year after the admin-entered actual end date of the school term incurred. Fixed semester-end dates must not be assumed; changing a term’s end date affects defaults for future INC entries without silently rewriting existing recorded deadlines. |
| FR-GWA-02 | INC completion before/on its deadline creates one unique INCResolution with completion grade and resolved timestamp. First completion is permitted on or before the deadline despite an institution-wide term lock or department encoding closure. Later corrections obey both applicable closures. |
| FR-GWA-03 | An unresolved lapsed INC retains stored INC status permanently; no automatic transition to Failed. Completion after its deadline is rejected. |
| FR-GWA-04 | Lapsed INC counts as 5.00 for the term GWA and as failed units for delinquency. For cumulative GWA, a lapsed INC retake does not replace or duplicate an earlier failing grade until a numeric completion grade is recorded, per FR-GWA-06. These are read-time rules, not stored grade/status replacements. |
| FR-GWA-05 | Completed INC displays **Passed** for completion grade ≤3.00 or **Failed** for grade >3.00 across Grades, History, checklist and dashboards. Display the original completion grade/provenance; do not show INC/Resolved as the current result. Keep the original INC attempt and its separate completion record for history. |
| FR-GWA-06 | A course cannot be retaken after a passing attempt. Each graded attempt remains visible in its original school term and retains its historical term GWA. Until a later retake receives a numeric final or completion grade, the previous failing grade continues to count in cumulative GWA. Once that later grade is recorded, it replaces the previous failure in cumulative GWA; the earlier attempt remains on the transcript, marked **Repeated** and excluded from cumulative GWA. Recompute cumulative GWA after the new result; do not double-count earned units. A non-numeric retake outcome, including INC or DR, does not replace or duplicate the earlier failure in cumulative GWA until a numeric final or INC completion grade is recorded. |
| FR-GWA-07 | Term GWA is unit-weighted using final/completion results only. Cumulative GWA is the sum of each included attempt's grade multiplied by its units, divided by the sum of those units, after applying FR-GWA-06 replacement exclusions. It is not an equal-weight average of semester GWAs. Displayed historical term GWA retains the grades earned in that term. Midterm grades never affect GWA. Cached values recompute after initial final entry, final corrections, prerequisite-grade overrides and INC completion/correction. |
| FR-GWA-08 | A service-role-only daily scheduled recomputation applies newly lapsed INC deadlines to caches and delinquency. SPA users cannot invoke this maintenance operation. Uncached reads always apply deadline rules. |
| FR-GWA-09 | Delinquency is computed per student/school term using failed and lapsed-INC units against the applicable curriculum threshold. |

## 11. Advising notes and staff remarks

## 15. Required acceptance scenarios

1. Admin provisions a student with canonical student ID (`YY-N-NNNNN`), program, active curriculum and initial adviser; department/College are derived. Adviser account requires College. Role and College/Department filters work together.
2. Blank grades on a new offering create one `currently_enrolled` attempt. Repeating the same new offering enrollment rejects the duplicate without a new attempt or audit write.
3. New enrollment with a midterm requires confirmation. Cancel writes nothing. Midterm 5.00/final 3.00 produces Passed; final 3.25 or 5.00 produces Failed.
4. Existing midterm 2.00/final empty accepts unchanged (or blank) midterm with final 1.75 and saves the final grade. Attempting to change an already-populated grade to a different value in Record grades (or re-submitting an existing grade in the single-attempt endpoint) rejects the request with no partial save and directs the adviser to Grades.
5. An unmet strict prerequisite shows Continue with override and Cancel. Cancel writes nothing; Continue requires a reason and atomically records the enrollment override and audit entry without changing the prerequisite grade. A separate correction to a failed prerequisite requires a reason and follows term-lock rules.
6. Institution-wide term locks and department closures block ordinary enrollment and grade/status changes. First INC completion on or before its deadline remains permitted in Grades and the Record grades pending-INC panel; later corrections obey both closures; completion 3.00 displays Passed and 3.25 or 5.00 displays Failed. Lapsed unresolved INC remains INC and counts as 5.00 only in calculations.
7. Latest semesters appear first. Back-entered older attempts/remarks do not replace the academically most recent passing attempt or reorder terms incorrectly.
8. Failed requirements remain retake candidates inside the confirmed horizon; students choose timing. Recommendations honor the selected actual offering term and display applicable badges.
9. Plans survive reload, retain student and school term, mark courses `In plan`, and appear on the student's dashboard without enrolling them. Historical advisers cannot mutate them.
10. Active notes appear in Advising and are readable by the student they concern and authorized staff. Students can retrieve their own archived notes but cannot retrieve attempt remarks or adviser-only AI.
11. Elective mapping targets only elective/GE slots and shows actual units. Discontinued equivalency rejects sources without another current curriculum membership.
12. Shift preview changes projected results only (applying exact course matches and approved curriculum-pair equivalencies); compare all persistent tables before/after to prove no academic write.
13. Cross-program offerings can be recorded while registrar enrollment and external approval emails remain outside SAAIS.
14. The current presentation provides `/docs` with curated documentation and a static API route/operation preview; it does not expose a `/docs/openapi` route or a live Redoc bundle. `/styleguide` renders the live design-system primitives.
15. A profile with Department Head and Dean assignments can navigate to each role's authorized workflows in its assigned scope. Neither combination nor prototype persona is an additional role; Dean and Admin grants remain exclusive to System Administrators, while Department Heads and Staff may provision the permitted roles within their department.
16. A provisioned person can use email/password or Google. Unregistered identity creation is rejected server-side; a valid invitation and password recovery for an existing profile remain usable. Password changes enforce 12–128 characters with uppercase, lowercase and a digit, require a different password, revoke other sessions and log an audit entry.
17. A student flags a grade with a structured reason and details; the current adviser corrects the grade with a reason or rejects the flag with a reply. The student sees the resulting status and response; a correction is audited, updates GWA/standing and is reflected in authorized grade views.
18. An adviser archives and restores an advising note. Its content and history remain retained; the student's view shows active and archived notes and never exposes staff-only attempt remarks.
19. A JSON grade import or CSV/XLSX student import is validated before confirmation (and legacy `.xls` is rejected). Rejected rows display reasons and no rows are saved merely by previewing; confirming valid rows commits them atomically with audit entries.
20. An adviser endorses a curriculum transfer with a reason. The Department Head can approve or reject it; rejection requires a reason, approval atomically updates the student's active curriculum assignment and carries over eligible courses, and authorized student/adviser views show the resulting history. Preview alone does not change curriculum assignments.
21. An authorized Department Head adds a curriculum-pair equivalence and credits an outside course for a transferee or shiftee. Duplicate mappings, invalid grades and nonpositive units are rejected; valid entries satisfy the target curriculum slot and record audit entries without inventing global course-code equivalence.
22. A Department Head or Staff user selects multiple students for bulk reassignment with an effective date and reason. Missing selection, invalid effective date or missing reason is rejected; each successful reassignment preserves previous adviser history and zero-gap continuity atomically.
23. Checklist, grade-summary, academic-terms and report PDFs show a preview before download, include the applicable letterhead marks and signature details, log a view/export audit event upon download, and do not modify academic data. Student checklist and grade-summary PDFs include all four approved marks: *Bagong Pilipinas*, *VSU*, *Faculty of Computing* and *DCST*.
24. In the current presentation, a Department Head can open a read-only student record from the department directory, but this action does not create a `View` audit entry. Dean and System Administrator student-directory rows do not open records. Notifications show unread state in the bell popover and Notifications page and never expose staff-only remarks.
25. Settings reject out-of-range or unordered standing percentage thresholds and non-`@vsu.edu.ph` sender addresses. Recording a backup date logs an administrative audit entry and does not itself perform or verify a database backup; production recovery follows NFR-04.
26. The Record grades grid enforces its `DR` midterm/final pairing, supports inline pending-INC resolution with a required reason, leaves unchanged pre-populated grades untouched, and rejects silent overwrites of existing stored grades so corrections always pass through audited grade-correction workflows.
