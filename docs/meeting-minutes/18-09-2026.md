## 2. Discussions & Updates

**Overall Design Feedback**
- Unanimous panel comment that the visual hierarchy and visual design are good.
- Mr. Pastoril remarked that, for an undergraduate project, the design quality was unexpectedly good.

**Dashboard Feedback**
- Mr. Barera suggested renaming "Currently Enrolled" to "Suggested (Future) Enrollment."
- Mr. Nierras suggested adding tabs to switch between "Current" and "Suggested (Future)" Enrollment views.
- Both Mr. Barera and Mr. Nierras advised placing the tab switch or combo box in the upper-right corner of the card.
- Mr. Barera noted that a Quick Access section is expected on the Dashboard.

**Team Management Feedback (for Project Manager – Lelis)**
- Panel observed a lack of system knowledge across team members.
- Mr. Barera advised better delegation of workload — large tasks should be split among 2 or more members rather than assigned to a single person, to lighten individual workload.
- Note: contribution grades are based on the number of commits.

**Feedback for Members**
- Members were advised not to wait for the Project Manager to assign tasks, and to volunteer for work in order to contribute more to the team.

**"Recent Activity" Section**
- Reduce the number of rows shown; add a "View All" button that opens a modal window displaying all rows.

**Attendees Tab**
- Remove the side navigation (Overview, Checklist, etc.), as it wrongly implies a global tab and is redundant given the existing in-page tabs on the Attendees page.

**Recorded Exceptions Section**
- Mr. Nierras requested horizontal scrollability.
- Mr. Pastoril requested a card view.
- Overall verdict: prioritize readability.

**Technical Design Note**
- When implementing the system, developers should roleplay as the adviser (end user) or interview a real adviser to understand actual workflows.

**Most-Discussed Concern**
- Mr. Barera, Mr. Pastoril, and Mr. Nierras all raised the same question: what is the purpose of the "Record Attempt" button?

---

## 3. Decisions Made

- **Decision 1:** Rename "Currently Enrolled" to "Suggested (Future) Enrollment" and add a tab/combo box (upper-right corner of the card) to switch between "Current" and "Suggested (Future)" Enrollment.
- **Decision 2:** Add a Quick Access section to the Dashboard.
- **Decision 3:** Project Manager to redistribute/delegate large tasks across multiple members instead of single owners.
- **Decision 4:** Members to proactively volunteer for tasks rather than waiting for assignment.
- **Decision 5:** Redesign "Recent Activity" to show fewer rows by default, with a "View All" modal for the full list.
- **Decision 6:** Remove the side navigation from the Attendees tab.
- **Decision 7:** Redesign the Recorded Exceptions section for readability (horizontal scroll and/or card view).
- **Decision 8:** Clarify and, if needed, redesign or better justify the purpose of the "Record Attempt" button.
